Recipes & Production

Ingredient amounts for each menu, so taste stays consistent and stock is easy to track.

Add new recipe

Pick a menu, fill in the prep time, then add its ingredients one by one. “Deduct stock” ingredients reduce stock automatically every time that menu sells in Cashier — enter the amount in the ingredient's stock unit, or use g/ml and the system converts it to kg/liter.

How it works: every transaction saved in Cashier automatically deducts ingredient stock per this recipe — for example, 1 serving of Iced Latte deducts Coffee Beans 15 g, Fresh Milk 120 ml, and Sugar Syrup 20 ml. “Extra” ingredients don't count toward stock. If a recipe ingredient is out of stock, the transaction is not saved and a warning appears — add stock first via Purchasing or Menu & Ingredients. Ingredients below minimum show up automatically in “Ingredients to buy” on the Overview.
